What is Funding Rate Arbitrage?

Funding arbitrage is a crypto trading strategy that allows earning passive income with low risk by exploiting the difference between spot and futures market prices. When the Funding Rate is positive, long position traders pay short position traders. The strategy involves opening a short position on futures and buying the asset on spot for hedging, allowing you to earn the funding fee every 8 hours.
