How long does it take to create a website?

Timelines vary greatly depending on project complexity. A simple landing page can be ready in 3-5 business days, while a large corporate portal or e-commerce solution will require 4-8 weeks.

A basic bot version with minimal functionality starts from 10,000 UAH. The price increases depending on the complexity of the logic, integrations with third-party services, data volume, and UI/UX design.

Do you provide support after the project launch?

Yes, we offer a full package of maintenance and technical support services. This includes monitoring, updates, backups, troubleshooting, and further product improvement.
